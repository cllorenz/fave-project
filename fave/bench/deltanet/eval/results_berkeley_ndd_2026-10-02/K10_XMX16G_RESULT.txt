WHY THE PREDICTION FAILED. It assumed a roomier heap would leave G1 under less
pressure, run fewer FULL collections, and so sample `max_heap_after_gc_mb`
only after young collections, which leave floating garbage behind. The full-GC
count barely moved: 38 at 8g, 39 at 16g. Whatever drives full collections in
this workload, it is not heap pressure at this size -- so full collections
compact to near the live set in both runs, and the figure is heap-insensitive
here. The live set came out 3% LOWER at the bigger heap, which is within the
noise of one sample each.

CONSEQUENCE: THE FIRST CAVEAT IS WITHDRAWN AND THE EXPONENT GETS STRONGER.
Recomputing the series on one heap throughout (16g):

                          mixed 8g/16g/16g    ONE HEAP 16g
    k=10 -> k=3                 0.548             0.573
    k=3  -> k=2                 0.582             0.582
    three-point fit             0.555             0.575
    k=1 live set               12.3 GB           12.5 GB

The two intervals were 0.034 apart and are now 0.009 apart. Removing the
confound did not weaken the sublinear result; it made the two measurements of
it agree almost exactly. `-Xmx 20g` at k=1 is unchanged.

THE SECOND CAVEAT STILL STANDS, and is now better supported. The exponent does
rise with size, and since 8g and 16g are now known to agree at k=10, the
2026-09-29 drill's 8g runs are comparable with these: 459,202 -> 2,317 MB
against this run's 1,351,800 -> 3,414 MB is an exponent of 0.36, below the
0.573 and 0.582 above it. Rising, then flat across the top two intervals --
which is the part an extrapolation to k=1 rests on, so it rests better than it
did.

AN UNRELATED FINDING, and it reinforces a correction already made. Committed
heap grew 6,384 -> 10,816 MB (+69%) while peak RSS FELL 8,436 -> 7,949 MB.
Committed heap is address space the JVM has reserved, not pages it has touched,
so it is not a component of RSS and "RSS minus committed heap" is not a
quantity at all -- it can be driven negative by raising -Xmx alone, without one
byte of real memory changing. K2_RESULT.txt withdrew the Python-side figure
derived that way on the evidence that it moved 44% between adjacent sizes;
this is the mechanism behind that.
