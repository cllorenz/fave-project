THE REVISION: -Xmx12g -> -Xmx16g for k=3.
  PROTOCOL.txt chose 12g from the only bound that existed -- the 19 GB machine's
  5g run, which OOMed with the live set at the 5,119 MB ceiling, bounding NDD's
  live set at 4.48M rules BELOW 5.1 GB with no upper bound.
  This run gives a far better estimate: live heap after GC 3,519 MB at
  1,351,800 rules. Scaling linearly to 4,476,240 (x3.31) predicts a live set
  near 11.6 GB at k=3 -- which is essentially 12g. A heap that equals its live
  set does not work: G1 needs headroom, and the 19 GB 5g run showed exactly
  what the approach to the ceiling costs (56.6 s of GC pauses against 5.5 s at
  8g, then OutOfMemoryError).
  16g puts the projected live set near 72% of heap. Projected peak: 16 (heap)
  + ~5.6 (Python side, 1,712 MB non-heap at k=10 scaled 3.31x) + ~0.7
  (streamed benchmark process) = ~22.3 GB, leaving ~9.7 GB -- still far above
  the 3,000 MB floor on a box with no swap.
  Not 20g: that projects ~26 GB and ~5.7 GB of headroom, which is too little
  margin here when an overshoot is the OOM killer rather than a slowdown.
