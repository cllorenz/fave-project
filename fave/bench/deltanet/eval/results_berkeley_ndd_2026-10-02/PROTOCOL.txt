WHY -Xmx12g AT k=3
  Three of the four 19 GB failures were killed by the MACHINE, not the heap:
  at 8g the live heap was 4.0-4.5 GB when the external floor tripped, and the
  aggregator peaked at 14.3 GB against an effective ceiling of ~16 GB (19 GB
  minus the floor). Only the 5g run hit a real wall -- OutOfMemoryError with
  the live set at the 5,119 MB ceiling -- which bounds NDD's live set at
  4.48M rules BELOW 5.1 GB and gives no upper bound.
  Here the same floor leaves ~29 GB, so ~14.7 GB of headroom instead of ~1.7.
  12g is ~2.3x the known live-set floor and projects a peak near 18 GB, about
  11 GB clear of the ceiling -- margin chosen deliberately over a tighter heap
  because this box has no swap.
