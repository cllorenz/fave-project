  k = 30's 1.96 per rule)

  NDD's live heap: 2.84 GB at 0.90 M rules (k = 30) and 9.2 GB at 7.4 M (the
    retry's partial build) give an exponent of 0.557 (NDD-Flood: 0.555-0.575),

    live data, the upper bound does not fit. Judged sufficient to try, with
    the heap cap fitted (the retry's 56 GB cap let G1 commit 41 GB for 9.2 GB
