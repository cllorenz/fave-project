PROTOCOL -- V5 campaign, phase C: the long BDD cells
Declared 2026-10-02, BEFORE either probe or the long run. Not edited afterwards.
MEASUREMENT_RUN_PLAN.md §5.3, §7, §9.

(Phase C's results go to their existing homes -- bench/wl_cloud/eval/ and
bench/wl_{stanford,i2}/eval/ -- beside the 2026-09 runs they are compared
against. This protocol sits with the campaign because it governs all three.)

WHAT IS RUN, AND THE ORDER, WHICH IS LOAD-BEARING
  The two 1 h probes run FIRST and the 24 h run second, so that if either probe
  says "re-run after all" that is known BEFORE a day is committed (§5.3).

  1. bench/faithful_bdd_measure.py --bench stanford --deadline-s 3600
         --status-every-s 60 --out bench/wl_stanford/eval/probe_stanford_1h_v5.json
  2. the same with --bench i2 -> bench/wl_i2/eval/probe_i2_1h_v5.json
  3. bench/cloud_bdd_measure.py --engine bdd --deadline-s 86400
         --status-every-s 60 --out bench/wl_cloud/eval/bdd_v5_20261002.json

WHY TWO OF THE THREE CELLS ARE PROBES AND NOT 24 h RUNS
  This is the triage's single biggest saving and its most reversible decision.
  Both faithful cells take nearly half the model in the first hour and then
  crawl, because the build is MERGE-DOMINATED (merge_ms/ppm_ms = 7.3x on
  stanford, 31.4x on i2) and the merge work that keeps the partition small is
  itself the dominant cost:

                    1 h probe            the long run           gained between
    f-stanford      4,245/9,491 (44.7%)  5,035 (53.1%) at 8 h   +790 in 7 h
    f-i2            86,709/154,974 (56%) 97,553 (62.9%) at 24 h +10,844 in 23 h

  faithful-i2's fixed-binary projection is 234-270 h. A 24 h re-run would
  re-confirm "does not finish" against a ~10x margin, and the only change since
  is a cost multiplier measured at 1.28x on a 3-router subset, where it changed
  no answer and no partition size. 1.28x CANNOT MOVE A 10x MARGIN.

  A 1 h window is also far likelier than a 24 h one to survive §6.1's unstable
  machine, and these two cells' whole information content is in the first hour.

THE DECISION RULE, DECLARED BEFORE THE PROBES RUN
  Each probe is compared at MATCHED RULE COUNT against its counterpart already
  in the tree (probe_stanford_1h.json / probe_i2_1h.json).
    - Within ~1.5x on rules AND ap_num -> THE RECORDED RESULT STANDS, cited
      with its date, its jar and the 1.28x caveat. No full run.
    - Outside that -> the cell IS meaningfully outdated and earns a 24 h run.

  WHAT THIS SESSION WILL DO IF A PROBE IS FALSIFIED, declared now so that the
  budget does not decide it after the fact: the owner is out of office with a
  64 h budget, and wl_cloud's 24 h run outranks either faithful re-run (it is
  the only one of the three that is both stale and reachable, and the only
  workload in the suite with an EXTERNAL oracle). So a falsified probe is
  RECORDED AS SUCH and its 24 h run is left for the owner, NOT run in place of
  wl_cloud. A falsified probe is a finding either way; running it would cost
  the one cell that cannot be reconstructed from arithmetic.

WHY wl_cloud KEEPS ITS FULL 24 h
  It is the only one of the three that is both STALE (S1: jar 92923bef of
  2026-09-27 against apkeep's a7eb8ad2 of 2026-10-01) and REACHABLE (79.2% of
  rules at 5 h, tail-rate bound >= 14.6 h). It has NEVER COMPLETED ON A CORRECT
  BINARY (S4), and it is the workload with the suite's only external oracle.

STOPPING RULES
  Probes: --deadline-s 3600, declared in advance, status trail every 60 s.
  wl_cloud: --deadline-s 86400 (v5's 24 h), status trail every 60 s, with
  bound_h computed off the tail rate -- so a crash at hour 20 still yields a
  usable LOWER BOUND instead of nothing (§6.1).
  A crash is NOT a did-not-finish. Killed by the declared deadline -> status
  deadline, reportable. Killed by the environment -> status interrupted, not
  reportable, re-run.

  The driver does NOT START the 24 h run unless 26 h of budget remain. A run
  begun with 6 h left and cut off would be a manufactured did-not-finish, which
  is the exact error AD6_PLAN.md §9.34.3 records.

PREDICTIONS (§7, declared in advance, scored afterwards, never edited)
  P7 bdd x wl_cloud COMPLETES within 24 h, between 15 and 22 h.
  P8 Both faithful probes land within 1.5x of their 2026-09-26 counterparts,
     so neither faithful cell is re-run.
     Falsified by: either probe outside 1.5x, which triggers its 24 h run --
     left for the owner, per the rule above.
