WHY EACH CELL IS HERE
---------------------
Three fixes landed after phase A. Each reaches a different set, and the sets
are declared here so that a cell cannot be quietly added later to make a
prediction come true.

(1) Q23, the no-U-turn rule -- f6836389. VeriFlow-FR forwarded a packet back
    out the port it arrived on; the other four engines never do. This changes
    what the engine DELIVERS, so it reaches every VeriFlow-FR cell: 9 `vf` and
    7 `vfplain`. (wl_tum_vfplain and wl_up_vfplain are in the deferred queue
    v5_phase_a_dnf.json and have never been run; they are not re-measurements
    and are not here.)

(2) LEGACY_CHECKS -- 4bdbe622. The retired probe-condition evaluation ran on
    every flow event. Removing it cannot change a verdict -- the compliance
    answer comes from NetPlumber::check_compliance's forward analysis, which
    never consulted the probe's `test` -- but it removes work from the measured
    path. A reported wall-clock has to come from the code that is reported, so
    all 9 `np` cells are re-run.

(3) verdict_valid -- this commit. `wl_tum` dispatches no check_compliance task,
    because it carries one universal probe and no checks.json; the old guard
    required that task, so all five of its phase A cells recorded
    `outcome: error`. Five fabricated engine failures in a throughput row.
    wl_tum_np and wl_tum_vf are already in (1) and (2); wl_tum_ad6,
    wl_tum_bdd and wl_tum_ndd are here for this reason alone.
