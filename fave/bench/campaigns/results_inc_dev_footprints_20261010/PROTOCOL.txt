PREDICTIONS -- declared now, checked in FINDINGS.md afterwards
  Re-checks are counted as the cells record them (`rechecked`, check lines),
  summed over the stream; "fewer" is against the default mode of the same
  engine, workload and stream in this run.
  P1  Every mode changes the same verdicts as the default on every update of
      every stream (the modes change which checks are asked, never an answer).
  P2  The three oracle runs pass (sample, selective == full, full == from-zero).
  P3  witness+dead-ends re-checks no more than the default on every stream of
      both engines.
  P4  witness cuts NDD-APKeep's re-checks on wl_cloud S1 and S2 by at least 25%
      each (in the calibration 55-56% of re-checked source-probe pairs there
      were reachable at the time; a witness should drop most of them).
  P5  witness cuts wl_airtel1 S3 (link updates) by at least 30% on both
      engines (a reachable check then watches only its path's links; 64% of
      re-checked pairs there were reachable).
  P6  dead-ends cuts wl_ifi S2 by at least 20% on both engines (83-90% of
      wl_ifi's re-checked pairs are unreachable checks, and S2's deletions
      move packets into ACL denies and drop sinks).
  P7  No mode raises a cell's median t_recheck by more than 1.5x over the
      default (building a witness is one packet per reachable query).

WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS RUN WORTHLESS
  A harness crash or an `error` outcome on a cell: recorded, not hidden, and
  the cell re-run once after the cause is understood.
