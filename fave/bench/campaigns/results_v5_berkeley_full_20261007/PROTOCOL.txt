CELL 1 -- APKeep-BDD, k=1, 13,402,846 rules
--------------------------------------------
  Through `bench/deltanet/eval/engine_run.py`, the entry point phase B's NDD
  cells used and today's BDD k=3 used, so all three are one harness:

    engine_run.py wl_berkeley --backend apkeep --engine bdd --keep-every 1
        --reuse-inputs --jvm-xmx 24g --memory-floor 2000 --deadline 86400

  NO 32 GB RSS CAP, because NDD's k=1 had none. Capping one engine and not the
  other would make the comparison meaningless. An OOM against 64 GB is a
  faithful result (§8.2) and is not retried smaller.

  THE HEAP IS 24g, AND THE CHOICE IS A COMPROMISE, STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN.
  BDD k=3 used 24g; NDD k=1 used 20g. Matching one means differing from the
  other. 24g is chosen because the claim this cell tests is a WITHIN-ENGINE one
  -- does BDD's live set stay sublinear from k=3 to k=1 -- and that comparison
  must hold the heap fixed. The cross-engine comparison with NDD is about peak
  RSS and wall, which are far less heap-sensitive while the live set sits well
  below the ceiling: 5,834 MB measured at k=3 and ~12.3 GB projected at k=1,
  against 24 GB.
  WHAT WOULD MAKE THAT WRONG: if BDD's live set is NOT sublinear and approaches
  24 GB, the heap becomes binding and the 20g/24g difference stops being
  second-order. B5 below is the prediction that would fail first if so.

CELL 2 -- NetPlumber, k=3, 4,476,240 rules
-------------------------------------------
    cell_run.py wl_berkeley --backend netplumber --keep-every 3 --reuse-inputs
        --limit-class v5 --limit-wall 86400 --limit-rss 32768
        --memory-floor 2000 --status-every 60

  limit_class=v5, the reportable class, with its 32,768 MB RSS limit KEPT --
  unlike cell 1, because the two NetPlumber cells it extends (k=30, k=10) were
  measured under it and this one must be comparable with them. The projection
  is ~24 GB, so a memory trip is possible; if it happens that is a reportable
  result ("NetPlumber does not fit 32 GB at 4.5 M rules") and not a mishap.

  Inputs generated per size in its own process, as §5.2 requires.

    Wall between 20 and 60 minutes. Two independent routes give that band --

B7  Peak RSS is BELOW NDD's measured 44,115 MB at the same size.
    Projection ~38.6 GB. This is the cross-engine claim and the loosest one

    Projection 17.1 h of load from a 2.504 exponent fitted to TWO points, both

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
--------------------------
NetPlumber at k=1. Already excluded on BOTH axes by the k=30/k=10 points --
~267 h and ~74 GB against a 64 GB box -- and nothing here changes that. If N5
fails, k=1 is excluded a fortiori; if N5 holds, the k=1 projection still rests
on the same two points and a further 3x extrapolation. It is not queued.

a run-to-run spread of 1.1% on NetPlumber at k=30, which is the only noise
