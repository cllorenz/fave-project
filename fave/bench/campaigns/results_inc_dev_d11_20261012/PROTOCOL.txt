LEFT OUT, AND A LONGER CAP
  BDD-APKeep on wl_cloud: its V5 build from zero took 3.24 h (RESULTS.md §3),
  more than the cap, so its cells would record no update. Its three A cells
  run with a 6 h cap; its 15 B, C, D and E cells are not run, as each would
  repeat a build the cap stops. The A cells' per-update rate sizes them for
  the campaign.

HOW
  bench/incremental_queue.py --jobs 8 --mem-budget 48000 (run.sh): in parallel,
  longest projected first; per cell an RSS limit, mem_mb = 1.3x the largest
  peak RSS of a dev cell of that workload x engine (peaks.json; oracle cells
  1.6x when no oracle cell was recorded; with no record 12 GB for NDD-APKeep,
  8 GB for BDD-APKeep, 4 GB otherwise; at least 2 GB). Simulated with the
  projections: about 55 h of wall time. Cells share the machine: per-update timings carry the
  interference of up to seven neighbours; that is accepted for an indicative
  run, and P7 measures it.
  Nothing else of this session runs on this machine meanwhile, and the tree
  (sources and build outputs) is not touched until the run ends.

STOPPING RULES
  limit_wall 7200 s per cell (21600 s for the three cells above). A cell it stops is `did_not_finish` with its
  per-update records up to then: the partial stream IS the indicative number.
  The other dev limits as incremental_cell.py sets them: limit_update 3600 s
  without a completed batch, counted from the first update on. A batch-"all"
  cell whose single batch takes longer is `did_not_finish` by it.
  A crash (`error`) or an oracle mismatch (`oracle_failed`) is a finding: it is
  not re-run, and it is looked at before the campaign protocol is written.

PREDICTIONS
  P1  No cell ends in `error`, and no oracle cell ends in `oracle_failed`.
  P2  Between 90 and 150 cells are stopped by their wall cap
      (`did_not_finish`); none is stopped by its memory limit.
  P3  Every E cell records at least one full checkpoint (selective = full =
      from zero) before it ends.
  P4  The queue finishes within 72 h of wall time.
  P5  Ablation: where the cells of one engine x workload x stream completed
      their streams, the re-checks summed under witness + dead ends are no
      more than under coarse, nor under either mode alone.
  P6  Batching: for every workload x engine whose S2 cells at batch 1 and at
      batch "all" both completed, the summed t_recheck at "all" is no more
      than at batch 1.
  P7  Interference: on wl_example, wl_ifi, wl_airtel1 and wl_cloud, the A
      cells' median t_total lies within 0.5x-2x of the latest earlier dev
      record of the same workload x engine x stream x footprint, for at least
      90% of the pairs that have one.
