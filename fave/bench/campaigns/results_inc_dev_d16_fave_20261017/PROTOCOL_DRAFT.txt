STOPPING RULES
  A limit stop is the cell's result with its prefix ("n of N"), never re-run;
  a cell at the 12 h cap is recorded as a timeout (owner). A machine stop is
  listed, not re-run within the queue, and looked at. A crash (`error`) is a
  finding, not re-run, looked at before the campaign protocol.
